# Product Blueprint

## Product definition

Private Ledger is a private, local-first, mobile-friendly operating ledger that guides one user through structured reflection so continuity stays intact and next steps stay actionable.

## Core principles

- Private-first
- Mobile-first
- Local-only data
- Fast entry flow
- Structured prompts over freeform journaling
- Searchable continuity and re-entry context

## Entry types

### Daily

Purpose: preserve continuity, detect drift, define next right step.

Prompts:
1. What was supposed to matter today?
2. What did I actually do?
3. What evidence of progress exists?
4. Where did I drift or fragment?
5. What state was I operating from?
6. What thread am I on now?
7. What is the next right step?

### Weekly

Purpose: compress week, remove noise, reset priorities.

Prompts:
1. What actually progressed?
2. What proof of progress exists?
3. What stayed noise?
4. Where am I now?
5. What bottlenecks kept showing up?
6. What gets reduced or constrained next week?
7. What is next week about?

### Monthly

Purpose: surface behavioral patterns and growth trajectory.

Prompts:
1. What did I consistently do this month?
2. What did I build?
3. What improved?
4. What regressed?
5. What did I learn about myself behaviorally?
6. What is next month about?

## MVP success criteria

- Daily entry can be completed in < 10 minutes.
- Re-entry after missed days surfaces continuity context immediately.
- User can recover last priorities and next step quickly.
- Mobile usage is smooth and low-friction.
